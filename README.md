## 🔎 Probe

**AI-powered research and intelligence**

Probe is designed to help users investigate complex questions, discover relevant evidence, connect findings, and turn research into structured insights.

Rather than treating AI as a tool that simply generates answers, Probe explores a more systematic investigation workflow built around retrieval, evidence organization, and analysis.

<details>
<summary><strong>Explore capabilities</strong></summary>

- Research retrieval, query expansion, and source ranking
- Evidence graphs and research timelines
- Structured investigation workflows and research dossiers
- Document-based research
- Collaboration and shared investigations
- AI-assisted interaction and voice-related components

</details>

**Status:** MVP preparation. The immediate priority is to validate the core investigation experience, improve reliability, and establish a usable initial release. Individual features may have different levels of completeness.

**Core principle:** AI-generated answers should be a starting point for investigation, not a substitute for evidence.

## 🌦️ Drought Intelligence

**Exploring drought risk through environmental data and simulation**

Drought Intelligence is a website exploring how rainfall and soil-moisture information can support drought-risk analysis in Ethiopia. It also aims to make drought-related information more accessible through multilingual interaction, including Amharic and Afaan Oromo.

<details>
<summary><strong>Explore the approach</strong></summary>

- **Environmental indicators:** Explore rainfall and soil-moisture inputs.
- **Simulation:** Test the prediction workflow under hypothetical environmental conditions.
- **Multilingual interaction:** Make drought-related information easier to access and understand.
- **Decision support:** Explore ways to explain risk estimates and environmental changes.

</details>

**Status:** Simulation-based development and evaluation.

Simulation allows the system to demonstrate and test its workflow, but simulated results are not automatically real-world forecasts. Predictive accuracy must be evaluated against appropriate real-world observations before the system can claim validated forecasting performance.

**Core principle:** Make environmental intelligence more accessible without hiding uncertainty.

## 🥑 Perishable Network Intelligence System (PNIS)

**A digital-twin approach to agricultural supply-chain intelligence**

PNIS focuses on understanding and reducing post-harvest losses in Ethiopia's perishable produce supply chains, with the current direction centered on avocados.

The project explores how farm conditions, transport routes, delays, storage constraints, and delivery decisions interact across a connected supply network.

<details>
<summary><strong>Explore the planned digital twin</strong></summary>

**Initial simulated environment**

- 5–10 avocado farms
- 2–3 transport trucks
- One cold-storage facility
- One prospective export buyer or export company

**Development direction**

- Combine suitable real-world research and geographic data with clearly labeled synthetic data.
- Represent farms, vehicles, facilities, and routes as connected network components.
- Simulate produce movement and investigate potential bottlenecks.
- Explore how delays, accessibility, and storage constraints may affect delivery outcomes.
- Evaluate simulated scenarios as reliable field observations become available.

</details>

**Status:** Under development. Existing groundwork includes geographic data processing, road-network analysis, data inventories, candidate-facility reviews, and fieldwork preparation. The complete digital twin and its operational predictions are not yet established.

Synthetic data will help test system behavior where real operational data is unavailable. Simulated outcomes will remain distinct from measured post-harvest losses and verified field conditions.

**Core principle:** Understand the system before attempting to optimize it.

## 🧩 The Bigger Picture

These projects approach different problems through a shared engineering philosophy:

| Engineering theme | Application |
|---|---|
| Evidence and information retrieval | Probe |
| Environmental data and simulation | Drought Intelligence |
| Geospatial networks and digital twins | PNIS |

They are currently separate systems, not a single fully integrated application. Their shared direction is to build intelligent systems that combine data, computational methods, and clear explanations to support better decisions.

## 🛠️ Development Philosophy

- **Evidence over unsupported claims.**
- **Simulation before untested real-world deployment.**
- **Transparent assumptions and uncertainty.**
- **Modular systems that can be tested and improved.**
- **Practical usefulness over complexity for its own sake.**
