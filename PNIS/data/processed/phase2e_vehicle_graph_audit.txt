IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- This is a preliminary graph, not a field-validated routing network.
- Vehicle eligibility is inferred from road class; private access, width, surface, seasonal passability, and truck restrictions are not fully available in the input.
- Track roads are included provisionally and may not support loaded collection vehicles.
- Road connections are made at coincident existing vertices only. Some legitimate junctions may remain disconnected.
- Centimetre coordinate rounding can merge extremely close vertices.
- Crossings without shared vertices are not automatically connected; this reduces false junctions but may miss real intersections.
- Bridge, tunnel, and layer tags are retained for review; they do not by themselves validate every junction.
- Distance costs use projected geometry lengths, not travel time.
- The graph is based on the Sidama plus 5 km buffer extract.
- A connected component does not establish real-world passability.
