1. PURPOSE
This protocol governs the collection and review of evidence for candidate
facilities. Map proximity and road classification are screening indicators,
not proof of physical access or operational status.

2. REQUIRED FIELD CHECKS
For each candidate, record:
- Visit date and observer identifier.
- Candidate location and GPS coordinates, including reported GPS accuracy.
- Facility type and whether the facility is operating.
- Physical entrance and whether the intended vehicle can reach it.
- Vehicle type assessed, road surface, usable width or clearance where relevant.
- Seasonal passability and any access restrictions.
- Evidence references, such as photographs or documented observations.
- Notes on uncertainty, unavailable evidence, or conflicting observations.

Record only observations actually made. Never invent coordinates, visits,
photographs, road conditions, or facility status.

3. EVIDENCE QUALITY
Check that observations identify the candidate, include a date and observer,
and have traceable supporting evidence where applicable.
Flag missing, contradictory, outdated, or uncertain evidence for follow-up.
A photograph or GPS point alone does not prove vehicle suitability.

4. REVIEW DECISIONS
Use these review outcomes:
PENDING_REVIEW - evidence has not yet been assessed.
MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED - evidence is insufficient or contradictory.
NOT_APPROVED - access or operational requirements are not met.
APPROVED_FOR_ROUTING_REVIEW - evidence appears sufficient for a separate
routing eligibility decision; this is not automatic routing approval.

The reviewer must record their identity, review date, evidence references,
reasoning, and any outstanding conditions. Where practical, the reviewer
should be someone other than the field observer.

5. ROUTING ELIGIBILITY SAFEGUARD
All candidates remain routing-ineligible at this stage.
Do not set routing_eligible to YES from proximity, road class, or an
unreviewed field observation. A separate controlled decision must confirm
the required evidence, vehicle suitability, operational status, and any
applicable access restrictions before eligibility is changed.

6. FIELDWORK TIMING
Conduct fieldwork after the checklist and collection arrangements are ready,
and before using these candidates as confirmed routing destinations.
Prioritize candidates using the existing Phase 2G.6 review sequence, while
coordinating visits geographically where practical. Record failed visits
and unavailable evidence instead of treating them as successful verification.

7. CURRENT LIMITATION
No real field observations have been collected yet. This protocol and its
checklist are planning and quality-control artifacts only.
